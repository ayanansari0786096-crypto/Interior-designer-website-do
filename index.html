The Studio

Design that begins with how you live.

We are a Lucknow studio of architects, interior designers and site engineers. Every project starts with listening — to your routines, your family, your budget and your site — and ends with a space that feels inevitable.

From a single room refresh to a full turnkey villa, we hold one standard: honest materials, precise detailing and craftsmanship that still looks right a decade later.

  • In-house architecture, interiors and structural team under one roof
  • Transparent BOQ and stage-wise billing — no hidden costs
  • Dedicated site supervision with weekly progress reporting
  • Vastu-aligned planning available on every project

What We Do

Six services, one accountable team.

Everything a project needs — designed, engineered and executed by the same studio.

01

Architecture Design

Concept to sanction-ready drawings — massing, elevations, planning and 3D visualisation for homes and commercial builds.

02

Interior Design

Layouts, material palettes, custom furniture, lighting and styling — delivered as a complete, buildable design package.

03

Construction Consultancy

Single-point execution guidance with vetted contractors, quality checks at every stage and a milestone-based timeline.

04

Vastu Consultation

Direction, placement and proportion guidance that respects tradition without compromising modern planning.

05

Structural Planning

RCC and steel planning, foundation detailing and structural drawings — safe, buildable and cost-optimised.

06

Estimation & Project Planning

Itemised quantity take-offs, rate analysis, scheduling and budget planning so you know the number before the first brick.

How We Work

A process with no guesswork.
